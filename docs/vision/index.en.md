# Motion and Facial Expressions

MediaPipe4U's motion and facial-expression suite brings Google MediaPipe technology to Unreal Engine. Use ordinary cameras, video files, images, and other media to drive 3D characters (digital avatars) in real time.
> In theory, **MediaPipe4U** supports driving any 3D character, including Mannequin, VRM, Metahuman, and ReadyPlayerMe.
> 

# Features

- Full-body/upper-body motion capture
- Finger capture
- Facial-expression capture
- Character movement
- Animation-data export (BVH)
- Camera capture
- Video (mp4) capture
- Image (jpg, png) capture
- Joint twist correction
- Animation smoothing
- Ground IK: keeps feet aligned with the ground
- Nvidia AR: uses Nvidia machine-AI technology to obtain facial BlendShape solving comparable to Apple ARKit
- M4U Remoting: facial-expression capture on Android devices
